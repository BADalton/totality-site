PRIVACY

Privacy Policy · Updated October 26, 2026

Overview

Totality is an orbital puzzle game developed under badhollowstudios. This policy describes the game and its support website.

Information you provide

When you submit a support or bug report, we receive the description, issue category, and any optional reply email you choose to provide.

Technical diagnostics

With your consent, the in-app reporter may include the app version, device model, operating system, current puzzle, orbital positions, selected gear, and move count. You can turn off diagnostic inclusion before sending.

How information is used

We use support submissions to investigate issues, improve the game, and respond to requests when contact information is supplied.

Service providers

Formspree processes submitted support messages. GitHub Pages hosts this website. These providers may process technical connection information according to their own privacy practices.

Advertising and purchases

The free version of Totality includes banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process technical information such as device information, IP addresses, permitted advertising identifiers, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising. Learn more through Google's advertising privacy information.

Where required, Totality uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent and provide privacy choices. A Privacy Choices option appears inside the game when required.

Remove Ads is an optional, one-time, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase. The US price is $4.99; other storefronts display Apple's localized price. A verified purchase removes banner and interstitial advertisements. Apple processes payments and purchase records. We do not receive payment card details from Apple.

Storage and retention

We retain support messages only as needed to investigate issues and manage support requests, subject to the controls of the service providers involved. Personal information is not sold by us.

Your choices

Reply email and in-app diagnostics are optional. To request access to or deletion of information associated with a support submission, contact us through the support page. Include enough information to locate your report.

Changes

This policy may change as Totality evolves. The updated date will reflect material revisions.
